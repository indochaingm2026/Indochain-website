Oxygen Rotary Furnace

New Chien Te Hang Co., Ltd. is the authorized agent of Italy’s SOGEMI ENGINEERING Srl, offering oxygen rotary furnaces that deliver innovative technology and high-quality equipment for the foundry industry. With a focus on energy efficiency and environmental sustainability, we are dedicated to providing customized services that help customers enhance melting efficiency and strengthen production competitiveness.

Equipment introduction

  • Oxygen Rotary Furnace: enhanced by oxygen combustion, it improves melting speed and metal recovery rate, effectively reducing energy consumption and pollutant emissions. It is especially suitable for the recycling of aluminum, copper and precious metals.
  • High-Efficiency Combustion System: adopting patented combustion technology with evenly distributed flames, ensuring stable melting processes and consistent quality of molten metal.
  • Automatic Control System: equipped with advanced monitoring and control technology to precisely manage temperature, oxygen ratio and melting parameters, achieving intelligent operation.
  • Energy-Saving & Eco-Friendly Design: reduces exhaust emissions and energy consumption, complies with environmental regulations, and supports customers in achieving green manufacturing.
  • Customized Engineering & Local Service: in cooperation with Italy’s SOGEMI, with more than half a century of expertise, Jian De Xing Co., Ltd. provides comprehensive support from requirement analysis, planning and installation to final acceptance, helping the foundry industry achieve continuous upgrading.
