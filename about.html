About PT Indochain Global Makmur

PT Indochain Global Makmur is an Indonesia-based trading and distribution company and a sister company of New Chien Te Hang Co., Ltd., Taiwan.

Supported by the experience, expertise, and international network of our Taiwan-based sister company, we are committed to connecting global suppliers with customers in Indonesia and international markets through reliable sourcing, quality products, and professional service.

Our business focuses on the supply and distribution of foundry & casting materials, refractory products, industrial chemicals, and other specialized industrial materials, serving a wide range of industrial applications and industries.

In addition to serving the Indonesian market, PT Indochain Global Makmur is engaged in international trading and export, connecting Indonesian products and industrial materials with customers and business partners in overseas markets. We work closely with suppliers and customers to facilitate efficient sourcing, competitive trade solutions, and reliable cross-border supply.

Through our strong Indonesia–Taiwan connection and established international partnerships, we strive to provide our customers with dependable products, competitive sourcing, responsive technical support, and efficient international trade solutions.

With a commitment to quality and professionalism, we aim to serve as a trusted bridge between Indonesia, Taiwan, and global markets, creating opportunities for long-term cooperation and sustainable business growth.

At PT Indochain Global Makmur, we believe in building long-term partnerships based on trust, quality, reliability, and mutual growth.
