Vibrator

NCTH Invincible Furnace Vibration Machine. Suitable for small furnaces. Adjustable air pressure makes the air volume more stable.

Size ranges

200–420 mm400–800 mm780–1300 mm

Product description

  1. The furnace vibrating machine uses the hammer head to hit the inner wall of the crucible, so that the particles of the lining material fill the gaps with each other, so as to compress the lining material.
  2. The furnace vibrating machine is designed with an automatic rotating device, which can be rotated automatically during use, and it can adjust the vibration force to ensure the hitting effect.
  3. Only one 3.5 HP air compressor is needed for use.
  4. No need to adjust the distance of the vibrator; it can hit from the bottom to the top of the barrel.
  5. Weight of the vibration machine: 12 KG.
  6. Increase the pressure regulating valve to limit the air intake and increase the life of the hammer (when the barrel is wider, it can be adjusted to increase strength).

Advantages

  1. Furnace quality assurance
  2. Improving the working environment of furnace construction
  3. Automatic rotation, continuous hammering, saving time and effort
  4. Uniform tamping, no particle segregation, high tamping density
  5. Adjustable vibration force and vibration frequency
