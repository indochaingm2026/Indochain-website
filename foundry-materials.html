Exothermic Piping Compound

NCTH Exothermic Anti-piping Compound is used in foundries after pouring, to keep the molten metal in the upper part at nearly the same temperature as the lower layer and prevent piping defects in the casting.

Reacting with the molten metal, the compound burns and provides a reservoir of liquid metal for solidification (liquid contraction) during cooling. After burning, an insulating layer forms to decrease heat loss. Cavities resulting from shrinkage become more curve-like (U-shaped) instead of funnel-like (V-shaped).

Direction

After pouring, apply the compound on the surface of the molten metal through the sprue and riser/feeder head. The compound reacts with the molten metal and starts burning to supply heat.

Application

Cast IronCast Steel / Stainless Steel / Alloy Steel

Advantages

  • Slower solidification of upper molten metal
  • Reduced heat loss
  • Improved feeding capacity
  • Decreased piping defect
  • Few toxic smoke or fume emitted

Grades

Exothermic AThermal insulation
Exothermic BThermal insulation & preheating
Exothermic CPreheating
NoteStore the compound in a dry, cool and shady area, away from any sources of heat, flame or anything that may produce sparks.

Patching Material

High-strength plastic patching material for repairing and lining ladles, runners and furnaces. Available in alumina/silica-based grades (R90–R80) and a silica-based grade (R66S), in drums and bags.

Used method

  1. Clean and moisturize the construction area with water or clay water, then compact the material by hand before smoothing the area with a trowel.
  2. After construction is done, air dry for 1–3 hours before drying by heat. Then it can be used.

Slag X Conditioner

SLAG X is a melt additive which alters the characteristics of the slag formed and purifies the molten metal, resulting in improved energy efficiency in electric induction melting.

Immediate benefits

  • Softened slag
  • Increased fluidity of molten metals
  • Less slag adhesion to furnace lining
  • Cleaner riser/feeder head

Subsequent benefits

  • Preventing bridging effect
  • Extended furnace lining life by 10% and up
  • Lowered pouring temperature
  • Reduced casting defects
  • Enhanced casting physical property
  • Improved melting efficiency

Direction

  • Electric induction furnace: apply SLAG X together with steel scraps and carbon additive or silicon carbide at the bottom of the furnace.
  • Ladle: place SLAG X at the bottom of the ladle, then pour the molten metal onto it.
  • Addition amount: 0.03%–0.08% of the molten metal. 0.05% is suggested for the initial use. Observe the condition of the furnace lining after each run and then determine the optimal addition amount.

Application

Gray Cast Iron / Nodular Cast IronStainless Steel / Alloy SteelCopper Alloy Cast
NoteDo not apply directly on the surface of the molten metal. Store in a dry atmosphere, otherwise the effectiveness will be affected.

Green Sand Molding Materials

Sea Coal (Coal Dust)

Sea coal (coal dust) is added to green sand to improve the surface of the casting and make demolding easier.

Specification

DescriptionContent
SpecificationF.C: 45–51%
Size150–250 Mesh
PackingBags in ton bag
OtherNone

Function

  1. Form a gas cushion: blocks molten metal from direct contact with the surface of the sand mold and prevents burn-on and burn-in.
  2. Create a reducing atmosphere: reduces the oxidized layer on the molten metal surface and prevents it from being oxidized further, avoiding burn-on defects caused by the reaction between casting and mold sand.
  3. Increase the collapsibility of the sand mold: makes the mold surface smooth and demolding easier, and reduces burned sand and penetration defects.

Superbond+

Superbond+ is a green sand additive that strengthens the sand, stabilizes the mold surface and lets more sand be recycled.

Application

Resistant Cast IronDuctile Cast IronStainless SteelAlloy Steel

Product characteristics

  • Increases green sand strength by more than 20%
  • Increases mold surface stability by more than 25% and improves the stability of the green sand
  • Allows more of the sand in use to be recycled and decreases the content of hot sand
  • Smoothens the sand particles, which in turn improves the blasting incompact
  • Bright carbon provides good circulation and improves air permeability, reducing defects when producing castings
  • Reduces water quantity, volatile and added material needed
  • Excellent collapsibility, reducing the time required

Addition amount

0.05–0.08% (e.g. for 1000 kg of recycled sand, add 0.5–0.8 kg).

Product category

SUPER A+For recycled sand that already contains Superbond+
SUPER B+For stainless steel and alloy steel
SUPER C+For first use, with a high concentration ratio
