Patching Material

High-strength plastic patching material for repairing and lining ladles, runners and furnaces. Available in alumina/silica-based grades (R90–R80) and a silica-based grade (R66S), in drums and bags.

Used method

  1. Clean and moisturize the construction area with water or clay water, then compact the material by hand before smoothing the area with a trowel.
  2. After construction is done, air dry for 1–3 hours before drying by heat. Then it can be used.
