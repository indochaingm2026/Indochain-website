About PT Indochain Global Makmur

PT Indochain Global Makmur is an Indonesia-based trading and distribution company and a sister company of New Chien Te Hang Co., Ltd., Taiwan. (見得行股份有限公司)

Supported by the experience, expertise, and international network of our Taiwan-based sister company, we are committed to connecting global suppliers with customers in Indonesia and international markets through reliable sourcing, quality products, and professional service.

Our business focuses on the supply and distribution of foundry & casting materials, refractory products, industrial chemicals, and other specialized industrial materials, serving a wide range of industrial applications.

PT Indochain Global Makmur also serves as the distributor of NCTH’s PLUS+ products in Indonesia, providing local customers with access to trusted products backed by the experience and expertise of NCTH.
